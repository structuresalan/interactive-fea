Interactive Finite Element Analysis (FEA)

Learn the foundations of structural engineering through interactive visuals and code.

1. The Foundation: Hooke's Law

Every continuous material can be thought of as a complex web of springs. The behavior of a single elastic spring is governed by Hooke's Law:

F = K × Δx
  • F: Force applied (Newtons)
  • K: Stiffness of the spring (N/m)
  • Δx: Displacement (Stretch/Compression in meters)
